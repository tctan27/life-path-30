# Life Path 30

A 3D, 30-year life simulator. Each year you pick one of three life cards, decide whether to protect your income, invest, and handle a mid-life critical illness. At the end you see your net worth and your happiness.

Play it: https://tctan27.github.io/life-path-30/

For illustration and education only. All incomes, costs, premiums, payouts and investment returns are simplified example numbers, not medical data, a quote, or the terms of any real product.
